# Outreach Email Prompt — Samuel Anyan

Use the verified company research, relevant job description (if available), master CV, tailored CV, and recipient's known professional details to draft one concise, natural and recipient-centred outreach email in UK English.

Do not invent recent company developments, recipient interests, job openings, metrics, achievements, or internal problems. If a specific hook cannot be verified, use a simple statement about the company's documented work. For speculative outreach, do not imply there is an advertised role.

## Required structure

**Subject:** [Role / Field] Opportunity – Samuel Anyan | [Verified Key Skill / Brief Hook]

Hi [Recipient First Name],

[Hook / Personalisation]: One short sentence connecting to their company or verifiable recent work.

[The Pitch]: Two short sentences explaining who I am, what I offer, and one specific documented achievement or metric from my CV.

[Value Proposition]: One short sentence connecting my relevant expertise to a plausible need or project, framed as a proposal rather than a claim to know their internal problems.

[Call to Action]: One low-friction, soft invitation to a brief conversation. Mention attached CV only if it is actually attached.

Best regards,
Samuel Anyan
[LinkedIn Profile Link] | [Portfolio / Website]

## Writing rules

- Follow Dale Carnegie principles: focus on the recipient's interests and value; be respectful, warm, clear and concise.
- Maintain the hook → pitch → value → soft CTA order, naturally, without showing bracketed labels.
- Do not stuff keywords or exaggerate achievements.
- Use specific, evidenced project results where relevant; never borrow metrics from sample emails.
- Use recipient name and company name accurately; do not claim to have followed their work if that isn't supported.
- If no verified contact or relevant opportunity exists, return a draft marked for review instead of inventing content.
- Return just the subject and email draft for downstream processing.
