# Proposal prompt

Write a concise, company-specific UK cover letter/proposal as a DOCX for {company}.
Target: {role}. Verified job description (if supplied): {job_description}.
Company context: {research_summary}.

Use only the attached master CV for candidate facts, credentials, experience and metrics.
Use company research to explain relevance. Distinguish public evidence from assumptions.
For a speculative approach, introduce Samuel Anyan, connect one or two evidenced achievements
to the company's work, suggest a plausible contribution as a proposal, and invite a brief
conversation. Do not invent a vacancy, internal problem, recipient, completed prototype or ROI.
For an advertised role, explain the evidenced fit to its responsibilities.
Use a respectful, recipient-centred tone and natural UK English. Keep it to one readable page.
Use the candidate's contact details from the attached CV. Do not leave placeholders.
Save a final DOCX that names the company and, for advertised roles, the exact role.
